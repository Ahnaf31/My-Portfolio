How Shopify Found Me: The Ready Prep Go Story

In 2022 a designer friend's Irish client needed a Shopify build. I had never touched Liquid. That project became my Shopify apprenticeship — and, eventually, my remote career.

ShopifyLiquidClient WorkCareer

A designer friend, an Irish client, and a Shopify brief

Around 2022, a close friend of mine — a UI/UX designer — came to me with a problem. His Irish client, Ready Prep Go, needed a Shopify site built from scratch. My friend was handling the design. He needed someone he trusted to handle the build.

readyprepgo.ie ↗

Why I said yes before I knew Liquid

He came to me because he already knew I could build websites. Years of running affiliate campaigns had forced me to become my own developer — every campaign needed its own landing page, its own product page, its own funnel. So HTML, CSS and JavaScript were second nature. What I did not know, honestly, was that Liquid existed at all.

I said yes anyway. Some projects arrive as a gift disguised as a deadline.

Learning Liquid on a live project

I built the entire Ready Prep Go store from scratch, learning Liquid the only honest way — on a paying client, with real stakes, at real speed. I broke sections. I fixed them. I discovered why section schemas matter, how templates cascade, when to use metafields, and where the theme editor's abstractions leak.

By the end of that project I was no longer someone who happened to know some code. I was a Shopify developer.

From one store to a remote front-end role

That single build became the portfolio piece that opened the door to a remote front-end role at Coalition Technologies — a US-based digital agency where I now ship Shopify and WordPress work for clients around the world.

Life is dramatic. You only get to connect the dots looking backwards.

A designer friend's favour turned into a career. It is a good reminder to say yes to the brief you don't yet know how to build.
